# T-Shirt Brand & Audience Research: NULLSET

- **Project:** IS117 Brand Studio — T-Shirt Research Checkpoint
- **Author:** Safwaan Arif
- **Brand Target:** NULLSET (The Minimalist Daily Uniform)
- **Primary Hypothesis:** Remote software engineers seeking an unadorned, durable cotton T-shirt with zero visual branding and high seated durability.
- **Secondary Hypothesis:** Tech students/interns needing a dependable, shape-holding shirt that survives shared campus laundromats.

## 2. Positioning & Audience Comparison

### Position A: Lifestyle Minimalist (Aesthetic Fashion)
- **Concept:** Focuses on clean photography, aesthetic coffee-shop vibes, and emotional lifestyle aspiration.
- **Risk:** Fails to address the specific practical fears of technical remote workers (e.g., collar sag on webcam, fabric transparency under high-lumen desk lighting).

### Position B: The Deliberate Uniform (Engineered Spec — Kept for NULLSET)
- **Concept:** Treats clothing as physical hardware. Emphasizes 240 GSM organic combed cotton, 1x1 reinforced collar binding, and pre-shrunk dimensional stability.
- **Audience Choice:** **Position B** directly aligns with *The Sage* archetype, replacing marketing persuasion with objective specs and verifiable durability.

---

## 3. Brand Identity & Honest Persuasion Application

- **Look & Feel:** Modernist baseline grid, neutral monochrome palette (`#FAF9F6` background, `#111111` body, `#2B3A42` slate accent), and clear Swiss typography.
- **Brand Character (The Sage):** Objective, deliberate, understated. Never relies on fake scarcity, fake countdown timers, or unverified sustainability buzzwords.
- **Cialdini Principle 1 (Commitment & Consistency):** Full disclosure of raw yarn weight (240 GSM) and collar taping so the user can verify durability before purchasing.
- **Cialdini Principle 2 (Authority / Empirical Proof):** Dimensional stability test data showing `<1.5%` post-wash shrinkage across 20 cold-water wash cycles.
